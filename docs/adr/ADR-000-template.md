# ADR-NNN — Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXX | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Phase:** the development phase in which the decision was made

- **Decision:** what we decided, stated in one or two sentences.
- **Context:** the forces at play (requirements, constraints, blueprint sections).
- **Alternatives:** options considered and why they were not chosen.
- **Reason:** why this option wins.
- **Consequences:** what becomes easier or harder; follow-up work; extension points.
